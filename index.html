Who we are

Local priorities. Sustainable progress.

Rwenzori Development Initiative (RweDI) is a proposed community-focused development organisation being established to contribute to sustainable social and economic development in Kasese District, Uganda.

RweDI intends to work with communities, public institutions, civil society, private-sector actors and development partners. We believe progress is strongest when communities help identify priorities and shape the response.
